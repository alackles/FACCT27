| 2026-10-08 | Self-deleted content (`_meta.removal_type == "deleted"`) kept in raw, excluded from analysis; in the processed layer it becomes a tombstone (ids, parent, timestamp kept; text and author nulled). | 2026-10-08 | Moderator-removed content (`_meta.removal_type == "moderator"`) kept and analyzed, flagged. | Acacia | Removal is the community's boundary-keepers acting, not the author retracting; what moderators remove is data about community norms. |

| 2026-10-08 | Text only: images, galleries, and linked media are not collected. | Claude | Scope limit for v1; stated as a limitation. |
